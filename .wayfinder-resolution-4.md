## Resolution

The user confirmed the following sign-in, membership, ownership, and session model. Application implementation remains out of scope for this planning map.

### Sign-in and identity

- Google is the sole sign-in provider for the first release. Direct Google Identity Services is sufficient; no additional identity intermediary is required.
- Tripper identifies an account using its stable Google identity, not its email address. Google token validation checks the trusted issuer and stable `sub` claim.
- Changing the email associated with that same Google identity does not remove accepted Trip memberships or change their roles. A different Google identity does not inherit memberships merely by presenting an old email address.
- Anonymous Published guide access requires no account.
- Account recovery uses Google's recovery process for the same Google account. Tripper does not introduce passwords, another recovery method, automatic participant promotion, or an administrator ownership-takeover flow.
- If a Creator permanently loses their Google account, the Trip retains that Creator. Existing participant permissions and publication state continue; another Participant does not acquire Creator authority automatically.

### Invitations and acceptance

- Only the Creator issues invitations, assigning the intended Contributor or Traveller role.
- Invitations target a particular email address and require explicit acceptance. They are not reusable public joining links.
- Every invitation includes a single-use secret link or code delivered to that email address.
- Acceptance requires both the emailed secret and Google sign-in matching the invited email. This applies to every supported Google account email type, including non-Gmail consumer accounts.
- The emailed secret establishes current mailbox access for invitation acceptance. It is not another sign-in provider or account-recovery method.
- Showing a pending invitation in My Trips cannot bypass the emailed-secret requirement.
- Invitations expire after seven days. The Creator may revoke or replace them; replacement invalidates the previous invitation.
- A pending, expired, revoked, or replaced invitation grants no membership, role, Draft access, or roster access.
- Acceptance is single-use. An existing Participant cannot acquire duplicate membership through another invitation.
- Accepted membership is tied to the stable Google identity. The invitation email is not its continuing ownership key.

### Membership changes

- The Creator may remove Participants and switch their roles between Contributor and Traveller.
- Contributors and Travellers may leave the Trip themselves.
- Removal, leaving, and role changes apply immediately to subsequent protected requests. Authorization uses current membership and role, not permissions retained in an old browser screen or session token.
- A former Participant loses member-only access and the Trip leaves their My Trips collection. A Published guide remains available under the same anonymous-access rules as for any other visitor.
- Content already committed by a departing, removed, or downgraded Participant remains.
- The sole Creator cannot leave or be removed through ordinary membership controls.

### Ownership handover

- The Creator may offer ownership to an existing Participant, who must explicitly accept.
- Until successful acceptance, the initiating Creator remains the sole Creator.
- Successful acceptance atomically makes the recipient Creator and the former Creator Contributor. The former Creator may then leave through normal membership controls.
- The initiating Creator may cancel an offer; the recipient may decline it.
- Offers expire after seven days. There may be only one pending ownership offer per Trip; replacing it invalidates the previous offer.
- Acceptance succeeds only while the initiator is still Creator and the recipient is still a Participant.
- Expired, cancelled, declined, replaced, or otherwise invalid offers do not change roles.
- Ownership handover preserves the Trip's identity and Public link.

### Browser sessions and FastAPI contract

- Successful Google sign-in establishes a Tripper-owned browser session lasting at most seven days from sign-in.
- Each browser session is independent. Signing out immediately invalidates that Tripper session without signing out other devices or the user's Google account.
- The first release does not add remember-me controls or a manage-all-devices feature.
- Use an opaque session identifier backed by server-side session state, delivered through an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie.
- Validate Google identity tokens server-side, including signature, audience, issuer, and expiration; apply the relevant Google Identity Services CSRF validation to the sign-in exchange.
- Protect cookie-authenticated state-changing requests against CSRF.
- Protected requests resolve the account from a valid server-side session and check current Trip membership and role. Role changes and revocation are not deferred until the seven-day session expires.
- Public guide responses and fallback snapshots exclude account, session, invitation, and membership information.
